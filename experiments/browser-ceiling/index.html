ICON-EU CEILING · browser experiment

Research only. This is a different model from the main briefing’s original ICON-D2 product. It is not integrated into a flight briefing.

The test fetches actual advertised forecast data directly from the public Source Cooperative bucket and decodes one ceiling field in a browser worker. It needs no extension, proxy or weather collector. Its legacy regular grid is due to be withdrawn after 30 November 2026.

Running loads about 41 MB of static decoder assets and one forecast file. The tested decoder used about 116 MB of WebAssembly memory; device requirements can differ. WebAssembly memory64 support is required. A failure stays visible.

The fixed public test point is LKCS: 48.9464° N, 14.4275° E. Heights remain metres above mean sea level. No local AGL conversion, observed ceiling, regional map or history is implied.
